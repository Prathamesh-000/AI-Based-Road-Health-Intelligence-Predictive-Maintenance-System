1. Problem Statement & Bottlenecks

Traditional municipal road maintenance relies on sluggish manual reporting and subjective contractor auditing. This causes critical breakdowns:

❌
Mass Frame Duplication: Driving past a defect at 30 FPS spawns dozens of duplicate tickets, overwhelming civic engineers.
❌
No Accurate Material Sizing: Crews dispatch with arbitrary asphalt loads without scientific 3D volumetric estimates ($m^3$, $kg$).
❌
Contractor Billing Fraud: Repairs are claimed using recycled stock photos, wrong locations, or sub-standard patch materials.

5. Dual-Mode Computer Vision Engine

Standard detectors fail in Indian road conditions where unpaved rural roads turn into water-filled pools. RoadSentinel AI dynamically switches detection modes:

Mode A: Dark Depressions (Asphalt) Applies Otsu inverse thresholding on drivable trapezoid corridors to locate cavities darker than pavement.
Mode B: Water Pool Reflection (Dirt/Mud) Detects sky-specular reflectance on brown mud using adaptive brightness $(\mu_{road} + 1.2\sigma_{road})$.
Vehicle & Bike Rejection Shield Ultra-sensitive YOLO pass ($conf=0.08$) masks cars, bikes, and riders. Combined with HSV Saturation gating ($Sat > 38\%$), zero vehicles or tires are ever tagged as potholes.

6. Closed-Loop AI Verification

Validates contractor repair claims using a deterministic 3-tier computer vision pipeline without subjective human bias:

Stage 1: Anti-Fraud Guard

• Haversine EXIF Geofence: Rejects submissions >50m from incident.
• pHash Hamming Distance: Detects and flags duplicate stock photos.

Stage 2: Planar Homography Alignment

• ORB + RANSAC: Computes $3 \times 3$ transformation matrix $H$ to geometrically warp contractor after-photo into exact viewpoint of defect.

Stage 3: Dual-Head Verification

• Residual Distress Check: Ensures 0 remaining potholes.
• Bitumen Texture Pass: Verifies fresh compacted hot-mix asphalt.
