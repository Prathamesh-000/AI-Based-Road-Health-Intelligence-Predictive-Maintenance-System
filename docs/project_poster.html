● 5. Dual-Mode Computer Vision
Standard detectors fail on Indian roads where unpaved rural tracks hold standing water. RoadSentinel AI dynamically switches detection:
Mode A: Dark Depressions (Asphalt)
Applies Otsu thresholding on drivable trapezoid corridors to locate cavities darker than road pavement.
Mode B: Water Pool Reflection (Mud Roads)
Detects sky-specular reflectance on brown mud using adaptive brightness: μroad + 1.2σroad.
Vehicle & Bike Rejection Shield:
Ultra-sensitive YOLO pass (conf=0.08) masks vehicles, bikes, and riders. Combined with HSV Saturation gating (Sat > 38%), zero vehicles or tires are ever tagged as potholes.
● 6. Closed-Loop AI Verification
Validates contractor repair claims using a deterministic 3-tier computer vision pipeline without subjective human bias:
Stage 1: Anti-Fraud Guard
• Haversine Geofence: Rejects submissions >50m from site.
• pHash Hamming Distance: Flags recycled stock photos.
Stage 2: Planar Homography Alignment
• ORB + RANSAC: Computes 3×3 matrix H to geometrically warp after-photo into exact perspective of defect.
Stage 3: Dual-Head Verification
• Residual Distress Check: Ensures 0 remaining potholes.
• Bitumen Texture Pass: Verifies fresh compacted hot-mix.
